# World of Aethelgard: Chronos of Fate

The owner's cast and regions. Each region (set) is one area of the game with its own town.
"In game" says who already exists in the build. Every named character's art is cut from
`art/raw/sheet_h.webp` into a sprite called `npc_<name>` (for example `npc_vaelen`), ready for their region.

| Set | Region | Town | Main characters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ash-Veiled Citadel | Oakhaven | Kael, Martha, Grom, Serena |
| 2 | Whispering Frontier | Sylvan | Vaelen, Kaelah, Lyra, Bran |
| 3 | Sunken Catacombs | Deepmine | Ignis, Valerius, Borgan, Nixie |

## The player character

The player isn't one of the named cast. At the start they pick a race (Human, Elf; more races once
they have art), a gender, and a name from a short list of easy names per race. Everyone starts as a
classless Adventurer and earns jobs in play (see ROADMAP.md, M6).
