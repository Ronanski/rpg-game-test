# Aethelgard: Chronos of Fate · Beta 0.4.0-beta.1

Thank you for testing! This is a **Beta**: the whole game is playable, but expect rough edges.

## Install
1. Download `aethelgard-v0.4.0-beta.1.apk` below and open it on an Android phone (allow "install unknown apps" if asked).
2. If an older test version is installed, **uninstall it first** (older builds were signed with a different key). From this Beta on, new APKs install over the old one and keep your save.
3. After that you never need a new APK for normal updates: the game updates itself when you open it (title screen shows the update status).

Prefer the browser? Play at https://ronanski.github.io/rpg-game-test/ (landscape).

## What to play
- **Tutorial:** six short tips on a new game.
- **Chapter 1, Echoes of the Core:** Oakhaven, the Old Cave and Gravemaw.
- **Chapter 2, The Sylvan Seal:** the Sylvan Wood (end of the south road, opens after Chapter 1), the elves, and Vyrnax.
- **Town defense:** build towers, walls and traps; survive the nights.
- **The archer:** Multi Shot, Piercing Arrows (Lv 3), Rain of Arrows (Lv 5), Fire Arrows (Lv 7). AUTO attacks for you.

## Please report
- Where you got stuck, lost, or confused.
- Anything that looked wrong: overlapping text, buttons hard to tap, art glitches.
- Fights that felt too easy or too hard (which night, which boss, your level).
- Your phone model, and the build shown at the bottom of the title screen.

## Known limits
- One hero for now (the Elf archer).
- Some Chapter 2 monsters reuse Chapter 1 art in new colors.
- Saves live on the device (no cloud save yet).
